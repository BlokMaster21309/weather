# Guide for an AI helping with this project

Before helping, read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Follow all project boundaries, student-authorship rules, and citation requirements in that file.

Most importantly: when you generate or substantially rewrite code, fence the generated region with comments that name the student's prompt, and add or update a plain-language entry in `citations.html`. Do not generate the student's entire product or choose its purpose and design for them.
